Why “Trembesi”

The trembesi is Indonesia's rain tree, and it is an unusual one. It is a large timber tree that grows fast, reaching full stature in years rather than decades. Its canopy opens like an umbrella, wide enough to shade a village square, and an entire ecosystem lives in the shelter beneath it.

It is also a powerhouse against air pollution, absorbing carbon dioxide at a rate few trees match, which is why cities plant it along their roads. And while its timber is not teak, it is strong enough to stand in for teak.

Fast growth, wide shelter, real utility, and a net benefit to the environment around it. That is the model for everything that grows under this name.
