Advisory

Private-market origination and deal advisory for Indonesia and Southeast Asia.

What we do

We work with global family offices, growth funds, and special-situations investors seeking exposure to Indonesian and Southeast Asian private markets — and with local companies and owners seeking the right capital or buyer.

Our work is mandate-based and relationship-driven: sourcing, introductions, and advisory through to close. We are not a platform, a fund, or a licensed broker. We are a US company with an operator's footprint in Indonesia.

For investors

  • Growth equity opportunities
  • Distressed and special situations
  • On-the-ground diligence support
  • Local partner and co-investor introductions

For companies & owners

  • Raising growth capital
  • Selling a business or a brand
  • Finding the right strategic partner
  • Cross-border structuring guidance
